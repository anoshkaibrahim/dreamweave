Frequently asked questions

  
Quick answers about working with Dreamweave. Need more? Book a free consultation.

  

    
How long does a website take?
A simple business site usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Stores and web apps take longer, and we give you a clear timeline after the first call.

    
Do I need an online store?
If you sell products and want orders around the clock, yes. If you mainly take enquiries or bookings, a business website with a form is enough.

    
Will my site work on phones?
Yes. Every site we build is responsive and tested on real phones, tablets and desktops.

    
What makes a site rank well in search?
Fast loading, clear content, proper page titles and a mobile-friendly layout. We set these up from day one.

    
Can you update my site after launch?
Yes. Our care plans cover updates, backups, security checks and quick fixes.
